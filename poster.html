Crimea · Russia’s war on Ukraine

Ukraine breaks the Kerch Bridge

A 227-metre road arch of the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea fell into the strait, and the rail line was damaged but reopened the same day. Russia struck Kyiv’s two Dnipro bridges and power plants, killing 14 people across Ukraine in a day and cutting power to about 700,000 homes.
Two arches of the road bridge stand; the span between them lies in the water. Train traffic resumed on 17 November, road traffic cannot cross.
Gulf · Iran

Drones strike Bahrain

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards hit a US-run radar at Sheikh Isa air base; Bahrain says it intercepted several. A third US carrier group reached the region. Trump said he was “looking into” a reported dirty-bomb plot; Tehran calls it fabricated.

Markets · US & China

Chip curbs, then a selloff

Washington put Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil under licence for advanced AI chips, closing the cloud route to China. The S&P 500 fell 1.9%, the Nasdaq 2.7%; Nvidia slipped 4.1%.

Climate · COP31, Antalya

Fossil-fuel wording splits the draft

A draft cover decision from the co-hosts urged phasing out fossil fuels and net zero for rich nations by 2030. The EU called the text untenable; oil producers resist. Talks end Friday.

Africa · DR Congo

Ebola outbreak ‘out of control’

Six months in, Africa CDC reports more than 11,200 cases and 5,500 deaths across eight provinces, with attacks on health workers and no vaccine for the Bundibugyo strain.

Asia · Manila & Shenzhen

Quad meets, a three-way summit set

Trump, Modi, Takaichi and Albanese held the first leaders’ Quad in two years, on maritime security and critical minerals. Xi, Putin and Trump are confirmed for a first trilateral in Shenzhen on Thursday.

Africa · South Africa

Two shootings leave 17 dead

Eight men were found shot in a minibus outside Pietermaritzburg; six men and three women were killed in a shack near Pretoria. Police call the attacks unrelated and are hunting the suspects.

Economy · United States

Nasdaq drops 2.7% as AI trades unwind

The dollar reached a 19-month high on bets the Fed hikes again in December, and Treasury yields rose for a fourth session. The VIX jumped 18.7%; gold and oil fell.

Japan: fighters scrambled after two Russian aircraft entered airspace near Okinoshima; Tokyo lodged a protest.
Indonesia: a magnitude 6.6 quake under the Banda Sea was felt widely; no tsunami and no damage reported.
Philippines: Marcos told ASEAN and East Asia leaders to turn energy and food pledges into deliveries.
France: new protests called as the presidential election nears; a francophonie leadership vote slipped a year.
