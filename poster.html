CRIMEA · RUSSIA’S WAR ON UKRAINE

Ukraine breaks the Kerch Bridge, then presses for air defence

A 227-metre road span of the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea dropped into the Kerch strait on 17 November; the rail line was damaged and reopened the same day. The strike followed talks in Kyiv with Washington and European officials on interceptor supplies. Russia hit the capital’s two Dnipro bridges and power plants, killing 14 people across Ukraine and cutting power to about 700,000 homes. Zelensky told allies the interceptors could not wait: they must arrive without delay.
Above  Two arches of the road-and-rail bridge stand; the road span between them lies broken in the strait. Rail traffic resumed on 17 November; road traffic cannot cross.
