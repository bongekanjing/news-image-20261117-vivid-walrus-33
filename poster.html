Crimea · Russia’s war on Ukraine

Ukraine breaks the Kerch Bridge, then presses for air defence

A 227-metre road arch of the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea fell into the strait; the rail line was damaged and reopened the same day. Hours earlier Kyiv had held new talks with Washington and European officials on ending the war. Russia struck the capital’s two Dnipro bridges and power plants, killing 14 people across Ukraine and cutting power to about 700,000 homes — as Zelensky urged allies to send interceptor deliveries without delay.
Two arches of the road bridge stand; the span between them lies in the water. Train traffic resumed on 17 November; road traffic cannot cross.
